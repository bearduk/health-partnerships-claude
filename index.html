Turning research into real-world health impact

Keele Health Partners (KHP) brings together health, academic, community, and business partners to tackle some of the biggest health challenges facing Staffordshire and the North Midlands.

Our goal is to turn great ideas and research into real-world solutions that make a positive difference to people's lives. By working together, we can speed up the journey from discovery to delivery — making healthcare more accessible and effective at supporting those in need.

Keele Health Partners is being launched in early 2027. This page is a preview — more information about our partners, research and programmes will be published as we launch.
